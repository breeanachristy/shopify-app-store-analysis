Analyst Memo — Shopify App Store Insights

Dashboard: Shopify App Store Analysis

Reporting Period: Latest available data

Key Insight
We have been getting more reviews in recent times than in previous years despite the amount of apps released being less
Business Impact
The apps we currently have are gaining popularity as evidenced by the influx of reviews
Recommendation
Focus on the apps that are getting reviews rather than creating new apps
